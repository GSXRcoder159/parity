## Known limitations (read these first)

1. **Held-out is only partly held out at day 0.** The scaffold's own tests exercised held-out templates #7 (`subscription-cancel-at-period-end`) and #9 (`refund-partial-then-rest`), and `docs/plan/impl/templates-validation.md` documents their behavior. So day-0 held-out does not measure generalization from an unseen template. From here on, the align loop never shows held-out templates to patch synthesis, neither the mechanical determinism check nor the LLM prompt. Held-out therefore measures **post-freeze generalization only**.
3. **Held-out exposure, W4 Task 4 (2026-09-26).** An implementer's context accidentally contained the validation rows of held-out templates #7, #9 and #12. The align loop never saw them, and the held-out score did not change across that task. They stay held out, and the exposure is disclosed here and in the sealed log (`docs/plan/impl/templates-validation.md`).
2. **Oracle v0 has 88 sequences**, not the planned 520. All 26 templates are in it, with seeds 1–4 for #1–#15 and 1–2 for most of #16–#26. The claimable sandbox key refuses every request (B1), so the 520-sequence oracle v1 cannot be recorded yet. When v1 lands, the curve gets the annotation "oracle expanded", and scores before and after that point are not comparable. The intervals below are small-sample intervals, and they say so.

## Noise floor and ceiling

The noise floor comes from sandbox against sandbox, over the pairs `align` uses for its noise keys: clean sandbox recordings of one sequence started under an hour apart. Today these are W0 runs A and D, 60 pairs, templates #1–#15 only, 28 minutes apart. The agreement is 100.0% at every level: L1–L3 [99.1–100.0] (420 steps), L4 [94.0–100.0] (60 sequences). So the floor is 0.0% and the ceiling is 100% for strata covered by #1–#15. This is in-sample for normalization v1.2, which was written from this pair. **Proration and boundaries (#16–#26) have no noise pair yet** (B1), so their scores above have no floor or ceiling. An empty noise set also means the align loop marks no divergence NONDETERMINISTIC today.

## Pre-freeze scaffold fixes (step 0a, commit `aba87fc`)

- `build()` deep-merges plain objects, while arrays, `null` and scalars replace. A handler's computed nested object such as `invoice_settings` or `billing_mode` no longer wipes the template's other nested defaults, so template patches under computed parents can take effect. Emitted events keep `data.previous_attributes` only when the emit carries it, as observed: only update events have it. Effect on this score: none. The set of divergences before and after the change is identical (10,808).
- `bench` against a target that answers as of `Parity-Now` sweeps events from `min(Parity-Now) − 60 s`, not from the wall clock. The twin's back-dated events are now collected. `bench` no longer sends `Parity-Now` to the sandbox: the sandbox ignores it, and the header would widen the sweep beyond the budget's projection.
