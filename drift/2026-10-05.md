## Triage (2026-10-05)

**Not API drift: an account-state change.** All 28 train rows have one cause. Between the 2026-10-04 noise-b run (last request 13:44 UTC) and tonight's slice (first request 16:09 UTC), the sandbox account stopped having a webhook endpoint that listens to invoice events, and Stripe ties both changed values to whether an invoice has webhooks to deliver. Every response that reports a `Stripe-Version`, in both recordings of every compared train sequence, carries the same one (2026-08-26.dahlia), and no status, error code or field set changed. The previous recordings were made on 2026-09-28, 09-29 and 10-04. The cause is inferred from the recordings and Stripe's docs, not observed: this triage had no sandbox access, this repo's commits in that window (the 2026-10-04 nightly's site data, align --assist tuning) touch no webhook endpoint, and the recordings show that the endpoint stopped counting, not whether it was deleted or disabled, or by whom. To confirm, list the account's webhook endpoints (Dashboard, Developers, Webhooks).

- **The evidence: `pending_webhooks`.** Every event in every train sandbox recording in parity-data, from W0 (2026-09-25) through the 2026-10-04 noise pair, carries 1 (6,431 of them in the train oracle this night was compared against). Every event tonight carries 0 (892). templates-validation.md ("Observed `pending_webhooks`") recorded the 1 from W0 on and noted that what it counted was never identified. webhook-endpoint-crud (#24) subscribes only to event types no template produces, so its endpoints never counted.
- **`webhooks_delivered_at` (16 rows, 50 divergences in 25 sequences):** null in every previous recording; tonight, on every invoice, its own `created` (`<t:~0>` on invoices made at the start, `<t:+1mo>` or `<t:+12mo>` on a clock's renewal invoice; 50 of 50). Stripe's API reference for the field: "This field tracks the time when webhooks for this invoice were successfully delivered. If the invoice had no webhooks to deliver, this will be set while the invoice is being created." Every train sequence in the slice that holds an invoice drifted on it (card-declined, clock-cancel-at-period-end, clock-declined-renewal, clock-period-rollover, expand-paths, invoice-send-invoice, subscription-create, subscription-interval-swap, subscription-update-price).
- **Renewal finalization, 72 h after creation, now 1 h (12 rows):** clock-period-rollover (#7, #14) and clock-declined-renewal (#3, #10, #17). The renewal draft's `automatically_finalizes_at`, the clock's second advance, which targets it (`status_details.advancing.target_frozen_time`, `frozen_time`), and the finalized invoice's `effective_at`, `status_transitions.finalized_at` and, on rollover, `status_transitions.paid_at`: `<t:+1mo+72h>` against `<t:+1mo+1h>` on monthly seeds, `<t:+12mo+72h>` against `<t:+12mo+1h>` on yearly ones. Stripe's invoice workflow page: "We wait 1 hour after receiving a successful response to the invoice.created event from all listening webhooks before attempting payment. If we don't receive a successful response within 72 hours, we attempt to finalize and send the invoice." The field reference: "Invoices are automatically paid or sent 1 hour after webhooks are delivered, or until all webhook delivery attempts have been exhausted." With the endpoint, `invoice.created` was never acknowledged (`webhooks_delivered_at` stayed null), so the sandbox waited out the 72 h; without one, it finalizes 1 h after creation. The draft's `next_payment_attempt` before any attempt was 1 h after creation in both recordings and did not drift.
- **`next_payment_attempt` after the declined attempt (clock-declined-renewal, 1 row):** `<t:+1mo+5d>` against `<t:+1mo+2d>` (`+12mo` on #10). This is the same 3-day shift, carried through: the attempt comes 71 h earlier, and rule `retry-day` still puts the retry about two days after it.

**No normalization gap.** These are the values Stripe returns for the account as it now is, and each sequence drifts once, on its first re-recording after the change. Replaying this night's drift on its 71 train sequences, against the oracle as it stood before the night (v1.7; the noise keys recomputed from train recordings only, none of which apply to a train template), gives the 28 published train rows: 80 divergences in 25 sequences. No rule is proposed. Writing `webhooks_delivered_at` as `<opaque>`, as `account_name` is (an account setting), would hide the twin's current null and a wrong delivery time. A rule for the finalization delay would hide a twin that finalizes at the wrong time.
