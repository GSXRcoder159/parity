## Triage (2026-10-02)

**Not API drift.** The 14 train rows are the kinds 2026-10-01's triage left open in normalization v1.6: timestamps one calendar month plus a fixed time, on sequences last recorded on 2026-09-28 and re-recorded tonight in October. A monthly period is 30 days from a September start and 31 from an October one. v1.6 writes a whole number of calendar months as `<t:+1mo>`, but a month plus hours keeps its hour bucket, which moves by 24 h. Every response that reports a `Stripe-Version`, in both recordings of every compared train sequence, carries the same one (2026-08-26.dahlia), and no status, error code or field set changed.

- **One month plus 72 h (11 rows):** clock-period-rollover (#4, #11) and clock-declined-renewal (#13). The renewal invoice's `automatically_finalizes_at`, `effective_at`, `status_transitions.finalized_at` and, on rollover, `status_transitions.paid_at`, and the clock's `frozen_time` and `status_details.advancing.target_frozen_time` after the second advance, which targets `automatically_finalizes_at`. The sandbox finalizes a renewal invoice 3 days after the period end, so these read `+792h` from a September start and `+816h` from an October one. Measured from one calendar month after each recording's anchor, every one of them is exactly 72 h in both recordings.
- **One month plus 1 h (3 rows):** `next_payment_attempt` before any attempt, on the renewal draft (`GET /v1/invoices` after the first advance, rollover and declined-renewal) and on invoice-preview-proration's preview (#7). `+721h` against `+745h`, 1 h past one calendar month in both recordings.
- **Retry day:** the clock-declined-renewal `$.data[0].next_payment_attempt` row also covers the second invoice listing, after the declined attempt. Rule `retry-day` rounds the retry to days, and October's extra day shows as `+35d` against `+36d`.

**Controls in the same slice.** The yearly-price seeds of the same templates did not drift (clock-period-rollover #18, clock-declined-renewal #6, #7, #20, invoice-preview-proration #14), because a year from a September or an October start is 365 days either way. clock-cancel-at-period-end (#6, #13, monthly) did not drift: its timestamps are whole months, which v1.6 handles. Every drifted sequence is on a monthly price.

**Held out.** The one held-out row (subscription-update-quantity, the preview's `next_payment_attempt`) was not opened. Its path is of the one-month-plus-1-h kind.

**What comes next.** These rows will come back every night that compares a monthly clock or preview sequence across months of different lengths: until the rotation has re-recorded the September ones in October, and again when November recordings are compared with October's (30 days against 31). That stops only with a month-plus-offset rule (`<t:+1mo+72h>`, 2026-10-01's note), which is not in v1.6.

**Fixed in normalization v1.7** (commit d3b6167). Rule `calendar-months` writes a timestamp at least one calendar month after the anchor, and not within an hour of a whole month, as the whole months plus the hours left (`<t:+1mo+72h>`, `<t:+1mo+1h>`), and rule `retry-day` counts a retry's days from the last whole month (`<t:+1mo+5d>`). Replaying this night's drift on its 72 train sequences, against the oracle as it stood before the night, gives 14 rows (22 divergences in 4 sequences) under v1.6 and none under v1.7. The same replay of 2026-10-01 gives 7 rows under v1.6 and none under v1.7. On today's oracle, score-twin gives the same L1–L4 and the same divergences on the train sequences under both versions.
