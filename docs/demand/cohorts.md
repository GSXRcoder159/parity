## Corrections to the research inputs

1. **Duckie:** the research cited "connect Stripe so your agent can resolve billing questions and refund requests" as Duckie's. On the YC Customer Support listing that sentence is Parahelp's description. Duckie's YC page never mentions Stripe. Duckie's fit rests on its own homepage (row 5).
2. **Fini batch:** the evidence doc says YC W22; Fini's YC page says Summer 2022. The evidence doc's "partial refunds, prorated downgrades, credit memos" for Fini was not found on usefini.com. Only the duplicate-charge refund demo is used.
3. **JustPaid:** the sentence "JustPaid integrates seamlessly with Stripe..." was not in the raw homepage fetched 2026-09-25. Row 20 uses lines that were present.
4. **Chargeflow:** row 25 cites only the page's `featureList` (structured data) lines. "Everything runs inside Stripe..." and "50+ sources" are marketing copy elsewhere on the same page — present on a 2026-09-25 refetch, so the earlier claim that they were absent from the raw page does not hold; they are simply not the lines row 25 relies on.
5. **Deskwoot:** fit lowered from 2 to 1. The primary page describes a human one-click refund.
6. **PostHog:** its `stripe-mock` service generates synthetic revenue data. It is not a test double for PostHog's own billing writes.
7. **Speakeasy:** the runbook's last committer lists another employer on GitHub. Contact goes to the Gram repo or team@speakeasy.com, not to that person.
8. **Cohort size:** 14 cohort-A companies score ≥2, 10 of them at 3. Only Autumn and Chobble have first-party public pain statements, which matches the plan's "Thin cohort A" risk (§10).
